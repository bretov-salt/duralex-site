Client portal

Your clients' case, on their own phone.

Clients sign in with a secure link to see where their case stands, upload documents, ask questions and book consultations, without calling the office. Everything lands in the right matter for your team.

See it from the client's side

Four screens clients actually use.

See it in the client's language

Tap the buttons inside the phone to try it yourself, and scroll the screens.

Everything about the case, in one place.

Clients see where their case stands, their upcoming hearings and deadlines, their balance and what the firm still needs from them.

Clients read and sign on their own phone.

The engagement agreement opens in the client's language. They scroll through it, sign at the bottom and can pay right away. The signed copy lands in their folder and yours.

The case folder, as the client sees it.

Clients browse the folders the firm shares with them and upload straight into the right one. Uploads land in the matter folder in your firm's own cloud, and the assigned staff member is told.

Questions answered, on the record.

Client questions arrive in the matter's inbox and go to the right person. Every answer is reviewed before it is sent, and the whole conversation stays in the file.

Consultations booked without phone tag.

Clients pick a time from the attorney's real availability. The meeting lands on both calendars with reminders.

Clients pay from the portal and see their balance.

The invoice, the payment plan and what has been paid are always visible. A payment updates the balance and saves the receipt to the client's folder.

Behind the portal

What your team sees when a client acts.

Upload

The file is saved to the matter folder in your own cloud, the assigned staff member gets a task, and the facts DuraLex reads from it wait for the team to confirm.

Question

The message joins the matter's inbox, is assigned, and the reply is reviewed before it goes out. Nothing is sent to a client without the firm's approval.

Booking

The consultation appears on the attorney's calendar with the reason for the visit, and both sides get reminders.

Status

When a court or agency date changes, the client's portal updates with the same date the firm confirmed.
