Where legal work gets done.

Five everyday jobs, played with sample data on the phone your team already carries. Tap a tab to jump in.

A new client, in their own language.

An inquiry comes in and the AI interview runs in the client's language. The conflict check matches name spellings across alphabets, and nothing opens until the attorney accepts. WhatsApp and texting are coming.

A court notice becomes a task.

The notice is matched to its matter. Tap Assign, pick the staff member and the reviewing attorney, and DuraLex sets the deadline and the follow-up.

Three checks, then the attorney.

Midpage checks case treatment, CourtListener checks the citation record, Descrybe checks each quotation word for word. AI supports the attorney's legal knowledge; it never substitutes for it.

On your letterhead, signed today.

The engagement letter and fee agreement are drafted from intake, approved by the attorney and signed on the client's phone through Google Docs eSignature.

Invoiced, paid, matched.

The invoice goes out by text or email. When the client pays by Zelle, the payment is matched to the client's account and the balance updates on its own. Automatic payment matching is a coming feature.

Questions

What firms ask first.

Does the AI make decisions on its own?

No. AI supports your legal knowledge; it never substitutes for it. Every fact shows its source and page, and nothing enters the record or goes to a client until an attorney approves it.

Where are my firm's files kept?

In your firm's own cloud storage. DuraLex organizes and reads the files where they are.

Is DuraLex quantum-safe?

Post-quantum protection is a coming feature. When enabled, connections to DuraLex will use hybrid post-quantum encryption following the NIST standards published in August 2024.

Can we text clients from our own number?

That is a coming feature, through business phone systems such as RingCentral, Quo, Dialpad and Zoom Phone, or Twilio for a number you already have. Texting is for client communication only.

Can we move over from our current system?

Yes. Early-access firms have their data moved over at no charge.
