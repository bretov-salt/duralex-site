Where legal work gets done.

A case from first call to paid invoice, played with sample data on the phone your team already carries. Tap a tab to jump in.

A new client, in their own language.

An inquiry comes in and the AI interview runs in the client's language (shown here in English). The conflict check matches every spelling of the name, and nothing opens until the attorney accepts. WhatsApp and texting are coming.

On your own letterhead, engaged the same day.

DuraLex drafts the engagement letter and fee agreement on your firm's real letterhead, never a generic template. Shown here on the founding firm's letterhead. The client reads it side by side in their language, the attorney approves, and the client signs on the phone.

A court notice becomes a task.

The notice is matched to its matter. Pick the staff member and the reviewing attorney, and DuraLex sets the deadline and the follow-up.

Court dates, on everyone's calendar.

A hearing notice is read, the hearing and the evidence deadline are set under the court's rules for the attorney to confirm, and both sync to Google Calendar for the attorney and the paralegal. Court email reading is next; court system, EOIR and USCIS connections are planned.

Letters and briefs, independently checked.

Letters of representation, motions and briefs are drafted from the file. Every citation and quotation is checked by independent third-party verification providers before the attorney reviews it. AI supports the attorney's legal knowledge; it never substitutes for it.

Invoiced, paid, matched.

The invoice goes out by email. When the client pays by Zelle, the payment is matched to the client's account and the balance updates. Automatic payment matching is a coming feature.

Why DuraLex

The four things a practice runs on.

Verification.

Every citation and quotation is checked by independent third-party providers, and every fact shows the page it came from. Nothing is filed or sent until the attorney approves it.

Getting paid.

Invoices go to the client from the matter. When a payment comes in, it is applied to the right client account and the balance updates. Automatic payment matching is a coming feature.

Assignment of matters.

Each matter and task has a named staff member, a reviewing attorney, a due date and a follow-up. The attorney sees who has what, and what is late.

Calendaring.

Court notices become hearings and deadlines on the right matter, on the right person's calendar. Deadlines are suggested under the rules and confirmed by the attorney.

Questions

What firms ask first.

Does the AI make decisions on its own?

No. AI supports your legal knowledge; it never substitutes for it. Every fact shows its source and page, and nothing enters the record or goes to a client until an attorney approves it.

Where are my firm's files kept?

In your firm's own cloud storage. DuraLex organizes and reads the files where they are.

Is DuraLex quantum-safe?

Post-quantum protection is a coming feature. When enabled, connections to DuraLex will use hybrid post-quantum encryption following the NIST standards published in August 2024.

Can we text clients from our own number?

That is a coming feature, through business phone systems such as RingCentral, Quo, Dialpad and Zoom Phone, or Twilio for a number you already have. Texting is for client communication only.

Can we move over from our current system?

Yes. Early-access firms have their data moved over at no charge.
