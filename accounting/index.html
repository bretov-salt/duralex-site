    In Pennsylvania, Rule 1.15(c)(4) requires a monthly reconciliation of each trust account that agrees with the total of the client balances, Rule 1.15(c) requires records kept for five years, and approved banks report trust overdrafts under Pa.R.D.E. 221(h). Other states differ. The attorney remains responsible for the trust account; DuraLex keeps the records and raises the alerts.
